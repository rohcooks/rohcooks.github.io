About Schemeo

Schemeo is a smart platform designed to make government schemes and benefits easier to discover, understand and access.

Instead of searching through hundreds of schemes, users can provide basic information about themselves and receive personalised recommendations based on their needs and eligibility.

Schemeo helps users understand eligibility, required documents, application steps and progress — all through one simple platform.

Features

Scheme Discovery

Find government schemes based on your age, location, occupation, income and personal needs.

Application Tracking

Track your application from submission and verification to approval and receiving the benefit.

Schemeo AI

Ask questions about schemes, eligibility and documents and receive simple step-by-step guidance.
