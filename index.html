About DAVV Connect

DAVV Connect is a proposed student-focused platform designed to bring the Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya community together. It combines university updates, college communities, academic collaboration, events and opportunities in one place.

Inspired by community platforms like Discord and Slack, DAVV Connect aims to make campus communication simpler. Students can discover important notices, connect with classmates, join discussions and stay informed about university activities without depending on multiple websites and groups.
